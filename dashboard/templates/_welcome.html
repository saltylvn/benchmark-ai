Why PMs fall behind

The day gets eaten by things nobody else is checking

Schedules slip quietly

Trusses that were never ordered. A sub whose insurance lapsed. A crew booked on two jobs Friday.

Benchmark checks every order-by date, certificate and booking each morning and flags it while there is still time.

Money waits on paperwork

A home dried in last week, but nobody asked the lender for the draw. A final payment waits on one lien waiver.

Benchmark drafts the draw request and the waiver chase the day they are due, with the amount computed by code.

Buyers and closings drift

Cabinet choices past due. A buyer who has not heard from you in a week. A closing the schedule can no longer make.

Benchmark reminds buyers, writes the update, and warns the owner when a closing is at risk.

Questions PMs ask

How it works

Does it send anything on its own?

No. Benchmark only drafts. Every email, Slack note, draw request and invoice waits for a named person to approve it, and demo visitors can never send.

What does the AI do, and what does code do?

Code computes every date, dollar amount and schedule check, and it is tested against hundreds of randomly generated teams. AI writes the wording and answers questions in Ask Benchmark, and every figure it mentions is checked against the data before you see it.

Where does the data come from?

The demo uses a fictional builder. For a pilot, jobs, milestones, orders and selections come in from a spreadsheet first, then from tools like Procore.

How much time does it save?

The demo estimates about {{ landing.hours }} of PM admin drafted on one morning, from minutes-per-task assumptions shown under the hood. These are estimates, not measurements. A pilot measures the real number.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. It is built for a PM between site visits: it works on an iPhone, an iPad or a laptop, and can be saved to the home screen like an app.
